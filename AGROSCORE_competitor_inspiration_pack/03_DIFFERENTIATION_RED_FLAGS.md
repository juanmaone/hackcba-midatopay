# Competitive Red Flags + Differentiation Strategy

## Claims to avoid

### "We combine BCRA + productive data"
Too broad. Many players combine financial and productive / alternative signals.

### "We use satellites for credit"
TerraMagna, SatSure, Apollo and others already do this.

### "We continuously monitor the crop"
TerraMagna publicly describes constant / real-time monitoring through the crop cycle.
SatSure markets portfolio monitoring using farm-level spatial signals.

### "We use AI to score farmers"
Traive explicitly markets proprietary AI scoring with 2,500+ variables.
SatSure markets a Farm Credit Score.

### "We evaluate credit from a CUIT"
Nera already offers a supplier-side credit evaluator starting with a CUIT.

### "We connect farmers with financing"
Nera, Agrolend and many others do this.

# Best hackathon differentiation

## 1. Stress-to-Exposure Engine
Make the user able to apply a productive / market shock and immediately see the financial consequence.

Input:
Drought -30%

Outputs:
Yield 8.2 -> 5.7 tn/ha
DSCR 1.82x -> 1.13x
Safe exposure ARS 76M -> ARS 49M
Decision APPROVE WITH LIMIT -> REVIEW / REDUCE

This is concrete decision support.

## 2. Explainable credit recommendation
Do not merely produce 82/100.

Output:
- maximum recommended exposure
- stress boundary
- top positive drivers
- top negative drivers
- lender action

## 3. Argentina-native stack
Possible production stack:
- BCRA: financial history
- MAGyP: historical yield / area
- NASA POWER / ERA5: climate
- SoilGrids: soil
- Sentinel / SAOCOM: parcel / vegetation / moisture
- local commodity prices
- Twin / ARGt: programmable disbursement if hackathon track requires it

The local synthesis is more defensible than claiming a globally new category.

## 4. Programmable action
If Twin is required, link the risk decision to a real financing action.

Example:
- approved line: ARS 76M
- tranche 1: released
- +60 day shock lowers safe exposure
- next tranche: requires review

The innovation story becomes:
"Risk intelligence directly controls capital deployment."

## 5. Product positioning
Do NOT position as:
"AI score for farmers."

Position as:
"Decision infrastructure for agricultural lenders."

One sentence:
**AGROSCORE turns parcel-level productive risk into an explainable credit exposure recommendation.**
