# AGROSCORE — Revised Design Brief after Competitor Research

## Product identity
Institutional B2B agricultural credit-risk software.

The visual language should feel credible to:
- credit officer
- bank risk team
- agri-fintech
- supplier extending credit
- structured-credit fund

It should NOT feel like:
- a farm-management app
- a generic AI SaaS homepage
- crypto trading software
- a gamified fintech app

# Visual style

Use:
- deep navy / charcoal shell
- white/off-white cards
- one bright agricultural lime or emerald accent
- orange only for warning
- red only for reject / critical
- restrained radii
- thin dividers
- dense but clean spacing
- tabular / strong numerals

Avoid:
- neon gradients
- glowing AI effects
- generic sparkles
- excessive green everywhere
- giant hero illustrations

---

# Core demo script

1. Open real/synthetic field case.
2. Show financial score 61 vs productive resilience 87.
3. Explain why recommended exposure is ARS 76M vs ARS 100M requested.
4. Click Drought -30%.
5. Watch yield, DSCR and exposure fall.
6. Explain top risk drivers.
7. Simulate +60 days.
8. Show action: review next disbursement.
9. If Twin is integrated, execute / block the next tranche accordingly.
