# UI / Product Patterns Worth Borrowing

## Target synthesis

Build AGROSCORE as:

**Traive shell**
+ **TerraMagna geospatial risk**
+ **SatSure parcel intelligence**
+ **Nera financial clarity**

Do not mimic logos, colors, names or exact component designs.

---

## Pattern 1 — Credit case as the primary object
Reference: Traive

Top area should immediately show:
- producer / applicant
- farm / field
- campaign
- requested amount
- status
- action

Example:

AGRO CASE #AG-2026-041
Marcos Juárez, Córdoba
300 ha — Corn — 2026/27

REQUESTED: ARS 100M
RECOMMENDED: ARS 76M
DECISION: APPROVE WITH LIMIT

This is better than leading with "AgroScore 82."

---

## Pattern 2 — One huge financial output
Reference: Traive + Nera

Make **Recommended Exposure** the hero metric.

The lender's question is not:
"What is the NDVI?"

The lender's question is:
"How much can I safely put at risk?"

---

## Pattern 3 — Map + operational sidebar
Reference: TerraMagna

Map takes 55–65% of horizontal space.
Right rail contains:
- score
- requested vs recommended
- stress DSCR
- primary risks
- decision

Map overlays:
- productivity
- drought
- soil
- vegetation

Do not overload with 12 toggles in v1.

---

## Pattern 4 — Exception / alerts panel
Reference: TerraMagna

Risk platforms become useful when they highlight exceptions.

Example:
- High financial leverage
- Rainfall anomaly -12%
- Yield volatility 18%
- Stress DSCR 1.24x
- Requested exposure exceeds safe exposure by ARS 24M

Use concise, actionable alert rows.

---

## Pattern 5 — Evidence, not AI theater
Reference: Traive / SatSure

Avoid:
"AI is reasoning..."
"Finding correlations..."
"47 variables analyzed..."

Prefer:
UNDERWRITING EVIDENCE
- BCRA history
- 8 seasons yield data
- 20-year rainfall history
- soil profile
- vegetation stability

Then:
DECISION
APPROVE WITH LIMIT

---

## Pattern 6 — Farm-level intelligence
Reference: SatSure

Use language:
- parcel-level
- field-level
- productive resilience
- rainfall anomaly
- crop progression
- historical yield stability

Avoid vague:
- smart agriculture
- AI farming
- intelligent crops

---

## Pattern 7 — Stress Test as the hero demo
This is where AGROSCORE should feel different.

Tabs:
BASE
DROUGHT -30%
PRICE -20%
DROUGHT + PRICE

Every click updates together:
- AgroScore
- yield
- revenue
- DSCR
- recommended exposure
- decision

The map can change subtly, but the financial effect is the hero.

---

## Pattern 8 — Explainable "WHY?"
Use a waterfall or signed contributions.

WHY 82?

+14 Productive stability
+11 Soil / water
+8 Historical yield
+7 Vegetation stability
+6 Climate resilience

-9 Financial leverage
-5 Delinquency
-3 Yield volatility

This gives the judge something concrete to interrogate.

---

## Pattern 9 — Financial Capacity vs Productive Resilience

Show both prominently:

FINANCIAL CAPACITY       61
PRODUCTIVE RESILIENCE    87

AGROSCORE                82

Key sentence:
"Productive resilience partially offsets a weaker financial profile."

This makes the concept understandable without pretending that productive data erases financial risk.

---

## Pattern 10 — Continuous monitoring as roadmap, not novelty claim

Competitors already monitor crop risk continuously.

Use it as:
"Phase 2: update safe exposure during the campaign."

Do not pitch:
"We invented continuous agricultural underwriting."

Instead pitch:
"We turn changing agronomic risk into an explicit financing action."

Example:
Score 82 -> 67
Safe exposure ARS 76M -> ARS 52M
Action: REVIEW NEXT DISBURSEMENT
